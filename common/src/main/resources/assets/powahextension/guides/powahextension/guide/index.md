# PowahExtension

Mekanism-style **machine upgraders** for Powah! Rearchitected.

Stop breaking and re-placing your machines just to move up a tier. Craft an
<ItemLink id="powahextension:upgrader_basic" /> upgrader, **sneak + right-click** a
machine, and it is upgraded in place — **energy, items, fluids and redstone settings
are all kept**.
